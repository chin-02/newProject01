6 FUN WAYS TO IMPROVE YOUR ENGLISH SPEAKING SKILLS

Do you want to inculcate and learn how to improve your English speaking skills? Do you often wonder if there is a shortcut to speaking English fluently? No such magic exists that will enable you to master English in five minutes a day. But there are certain measures through which you can learn how to manifest delightful daily English conversations, in a short span of time. Here are a few English speaking tips that will guide you accordingly:

1. BELIEVE IN YOURSELF

I have a lot of people in my life who usually talk to me in English, but I was always hesitant to speak the language. I had certain barriers holding me back. “I am afraid to speak. I am scared people would make fun of me. They will judge me.” One day, I thought if so many people can, even I can. So I intentionally spoke in English and my confidence increased.” The key to an effective English conversation is to believe in you and to be confident. No human is born with an innate ability to speak English

2. THINK IN ENGLISH

Sometimes the difficult thing about conversing in a new language isn’t the language itself, but how you think about it. Often people think in their native language and then try to speak English, implying that you’d always have to translate between languages. Translating isn’t an easy thing to do! Even people fluent in two or more languages have trouble switching between languages. The key is to think in English.


You can do this anywhere, anytime. Try to use English when you’re thinking about your day, or when you’re trying to decide what food to order. Thinking is prerequisite to any verbal conversation; English conversation practise with your own self is the most constructive way to familiarize yourself with the language. You’ll notice that when you think in English, it’s easier for you to speak in English. For instance; if you want to say the word “apple” in English, for example, right now you probably think of the word in your native language first, and then try to think of the correct word in English. Instead, try imagining a picture of an apple, and then just think the English word “apple”.Real fluency happens when you stop mentally translating conversations.


3. TAKE A DEEP END DIVE

Studying English for an hour once a week isn’t usually enough to make any real progress. The best way to quickly improve your English is to spend at least a few minutes practising every day. Immerse yourself as much as possible every time you study, and challenge yourself to listen to, read, and even say things in English that you think might be too difficult for you. If you want to speak English fluently, you need to make it an essential part of your everyday life.


The right attitude can make the difference between failure and success. Stop thinking of yourself as someone who is learning English, and start thinking of yourself as someone who speaks English. It’s a small change, but it will make you feel more confident and help you to use the English you already know more effectively. Whenever you can, take a few minutes out of your day to stand in front of the mirror and speak. Choose a topic, set a timer for two to three minutes and simply, talk. The point of this exercise is to watch your mouth, face and body language as you speak. It also makes you feel like you’re talking to someone; thereby you pretend you’re having a conversation with a mate.
